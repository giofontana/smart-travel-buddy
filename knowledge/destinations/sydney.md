# Sydney

## Overview
Australia's largest city wraps around stunning harbour. Beach culture meets urban sophistication. Expensive city with high quality of life. English speaking with Aussie slang. Laid-back attitude despite size. Weather dominates lifestyle - outdoor dining and beaches central to culture.

## Best Time to Visit
September-November (spring) or March-May (autumn) for mild weather. December-February is summer - hot, crowded beaches, high prices. Winter (June-August) mild but can be rainy. Christmas and New Year extremely expensive.

## Neighborhoods
CBD: Opera House, Harbour Bridge, Circular Quay. The Rocks: Historic, weekend markets. Darling Harbour: Tourist attractions, restaurants. Bondi/Coogee: Beach culture, coastal walks. Newtown: Alternative, students, cafes. Surry Hills: Trendy dining, bars.

## Transportation
Trains, buses, ferries use Opal card or contactless payment. Ferries to Manly offer scenic harbour crossing. Trains connect beaches and suburbs. Walking pleasant in centre. Taxis expensive - Uber common. Car unnecessary in city. Sydney Airport train has hefty surcharge.

## Must-See Spots
Opera House tour, Harbour Bridge climb or walk, Bondi to Coogee coastal walk, Royal Botanic Gardens, Taronga Zoo, ferry to Manly Beach. Brunch culture strong - try flat white coffee. Meat pies, Tim Tams, fish and chips at beach. Markets on weekends. Pub culture for sports viewing.
