# London

## Overview
Sprawling city divided by the Thames River and organized into boroughs. Central London covers Westminster, City of London, West End. Mix of historic landmarks and modern culture. English spoken but diverse accents. Expensive compared to most cities. Weather unpredictable - always carry layers.

## Best Time to Visit
May-June or September for mild weather and festivals. July-August warm but crowded. Winter dark and cold but magical with Christmas lights. Spring sees parks bloom. Book accommodation early for summer.

## Neighborhoods
Westminster: Parliament, Big Ben, Buckingham Palace. West End: Theater, shopping, Soho nightlife. South Bank: Thames walks, markets, Tate Modern. Camden: Markets, alternative culture. Shoreditch: Hipster East London. Notting Hill: Portobello Market, colorful houses.

## Transportation
Tube (Underground) is extensive - get Oyster card or use contactless payment. Buses offer sightseeing (sit upstairs). Overground and trains connect outer areas. Walking along Thames recommended. Black cabs iconic but pricey. Bike hire available. Congestion charges for driving.

## Must-See Spots
British Museum (free), Tower of London, Westminster Abbey, Changing of Guard at Buckingham Palace, Tate galleries (free), Borough Market for food, Hyde Park. Afternoon tea is touristy but fun. Pub culture essential - try traditional Sunday roast. West End shows rival Broadway. Many museums free.
