# Rome

## Overview
Ancient ruins mix with baroque churches and modern life in this chaotic, charming city. Built on seven hills with narrow cobblestone streets. Cars and scooters dominate traffic. Most major sites walkable in historic center. Italian required less than expected in tourist areas.

## Best Time to Visit
April-May or September-October for mild weather. Avoid August when locals flee and heat is oppressive. Winter is quiet but some sites have shorter hours. Easter brings massive crowds but special celebrations.

## Neighborhoods
Centro Storico: Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Trevi Fountain. Trastevere: Cobblestone charm, nightlife, restaurants. Vatican area: St. Peter's, museums. Monti: Bohemian, cafes, vintage shops. Testaccio: Food market, authentic Roman cuisine.

## Transportation
Walking is primary for center. Metro has only 3 lines but connects key sites. Buses extensive but confusing. Taxis available - use official white cabs or apps. Roma Pass offers transport and museum entry. Most streets not wheelchair accessible.

## Must-See Spots
Colosseum and Roman Forum (book timed entry), Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel (book ahead, dress modestly), Pantheon (free), Spanish Steps, Villa Borghese gardens. Eat cacio e pepe, carbonara, and pizza al taglio. Gelato from small artisan shops, not chains. Aperitivo hour offers buffet with drinks.
