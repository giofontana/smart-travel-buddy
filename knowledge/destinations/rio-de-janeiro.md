# Rio de Janeiro

## Overview
Brazil's beach metropolis famous for Christ statue, carnival, and carioca culture. Portuguese spoken - limited English outside tourist areas. Dramatic landscape with beaches, mountains, favelas. Safety requires awareness. Extremely friendly locals. Music and beach life define the culture. Very affordable.

## Best Time to Visit
December-March for summer and Carnival (February/March) but hot and crowded. September-November for spring weather and fewer tourists. Avoid June-August winter - cooler and rainier. Book far ahead for Carnival. Beach culture year-round.

## Neighborhoods
Copacabana: Iconic beach, hotels, touristy. Ipanema/Leblon: Upscale, trendy, better beach. Santa Teresa: Bohemian hills, trams, art. Centro: Historic downtown, business. Lapa: Nightlife, samba, arches. Barra da Tijuca: Modern, long beach, Olympic sites.

## Transportation
Metro limited but connects beaches to Centro. Buses extensive but confusing. Uber and 99 taxi apps essential and cheap. Official yellow taxis okay. Walking fine in beach neighborhoods during day. Avoid isolated areas at night. Cars face heavy traffic.

## Must-See Spots
Christ the Redeemer (book online, go early for light and fewer crowds), Sugarloaf Mountain cable car, Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, Escadaria Selaron steps, Lapa nightlife for live samba. Try feijoada, acai bowls, caipirinhas, pao de queijo. Beach culture: rent chair and umbrella. Football match if possible.
