# Paris

## Overview
Paris is divided into 20 arrondissements (districts) spiraling clockwise from the center. Each has distinct character. The Seine River divides Right Bank (shopping, nightlife) from Left Bank (cafes, universities). Walking is often fastest for short distances.

## Best Time to Visit
April-June or September-October for pleasant weather and fewer crowds. August sees many locals leave and some shops close. December offers Christmas markets but can be cold and gray.

## Neighborhoods
1st-4th: Historic center, museums, Notre-Dame. 5th-6th: Latin Quarter, student life, cafes. 7th: Eiffel Tower, elegant residential. 9th: Opera, department stores. 18th: Montmartre, artists, Sacre-Coeur. Marais (3rd-4th): Trendy, historic Jewish quarter.

## Transportation
Metro is extensive and runs until 1am (2am weekends). Buy carnet (10 tickets) or Navigo pass. RER trains connect suburbs. Velib bike-share available. Central areas walkable. Taxis expensive; Uber works.

## Must-See Spots
Eiffel Tower (book online to skip lines), Louvre (Wednesday/Friday evenings less crowded), Musee d'Orsay, Notre-Dame exterior, Sainte-Chapelle stained glass, Luxembourg Gardens. Eat croissants at local boulangeries, try bistros in residential areas, visit food markets like Marche d'Aligre.
