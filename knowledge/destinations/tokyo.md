# Tokyo

## Overview
Tokyo is Japan's capital and a massive metropolis blending ultra-modern with traditional. Home to 14 million people, it's organized into distinct neighborhoods (called ku or wards) connected by the world's most efficient train system. English signage is improving but limited outside tourist areas.

## Best Time to Visit
March-April for cherry blossoms or October-November for autumn colors and mild weather. Avoid Golden Week (late April/early May) and August (extremely humid and crowded). Winter is cold but clear.

## Neighborhoods
Shinjuku: Business and entertainment hub with neon-lit nightlife and skyscrapers. Shibuya: Youth culture, famous crossing, shopping. Asakusa: Historic temples and traditional atmosphere. Harajuku: Fashion and quirky culture. Akihabara: Electronics and anime. Ginza: Upscale shopping.

## Transportation
Get a Suica or Pasmo card immediately - rechargeable IC cards for trains, buses, and convenience stores. JR Yamanote Line circles the city. Trains stop around midnight. Taxis are expensive. Google Maps works perfectly for transit routing.

## Must-See Spots
Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa, Meiji Shrine, Tsukiji Outer Market for breakfast, teamLab Borderless digital art museum, observation decks at Tokyo Skytree or Metropolitan Government Building (free). Try authentic ramen in tiny shops, conveyor belt sushi, and izakaya pubs.
