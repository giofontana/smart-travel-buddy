# New York City

## Overview
Five boroughs: Manhattan (main tourist area), Brooklyn (hipster neighborhoods, food scene), Queens (diversity, airports), Bronx (Yankees, zoo), Staten Island (ferry views). Grid system above 14th Street makes Manhattan navigation easy. Fast-paced, walkable, 24-hour city.

## Best Time to Visit
September-November for fall colors and comfortable weather, or late April-June. Summer is hot and humid. Winter can be magical with holiday lights but bitterly cold. Avoid major holidays unless that's the goal.

## Neighborhoods
Midtown: Times Square, Broadway, Rockefeller Center. Upper West Side: Museums, Central Park. Village: Bohemian, cafes, NYU. Chelsea: High Line, galleries. Williamsburg/DUMBO Brooklyn: Hipster food and views. Financial District: 9/11 Memorial, historic downtown.

## Transportation
Subway runs 24/7 on most lines. Get MetroCard or use contactless payment. Express vs local trains matter. Walking is common - comfortable shoes essential. Yellow cabs everywhere but Uber/Lyft often cheaper. Don't rent a car.

## Must-See Spots
Central Park, Metropolitan Museum, MoMA, Brooklyn Bridge walk, High Line park, 9/11 Memorial, Ellis Island. See a Broadway show, eat pizza (Joe's, Prince Street), bagels (Russ & Daughters), diverse food in Queens. Visit observation decks: Top of the Rock or One World Trade Center.
