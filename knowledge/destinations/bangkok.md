# Bangkok

## Overview
Chaotic, vibrant capital of Thailand. Mix of ornate temples, modern malls, street food, and nightlife. Traffic notorious - plan extra time. Humid year-round. Very affordable. English widely spoken in tourist areas. Extremely foreigner-friendly. Markets and street food define the culture.

## Best Time to Visit
November-February for cooler, dry weather. March-May extremely hot. June-October rainy season but showers usually brief. Avoid Songkran (mid-April water festival) unless you enjoy city-wide water fights.

## Neighborhoods
Old City: Grand Palace, Wat Pho, historic temples. Khao San Road: Backpacker central. Sukhumvit: Modern, expat area, nightlife. Silom: Business district, night markets. Chinatown: Street food heaven. Thonburi: Quieter, canals, local life.

## Transportation
BTS Skytrain and MRT subway avoid traffic - get Rabbit card. Taxis cheap but use meter or Grab app. Tuk-tuks fun but negotiate price first. River boats along Chao Phraya offer scenic transport. Walking difficult due to heat and broken sidewalks. Motorbike taxis for short distances.

## Must-See Spots
Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew (dress modestly), Wat Pho reclining Buddha, Wat Arun, floating markets (Damnoen Saduak touristy, Amphawa more authentic), Chatuchak weekend market. Street food essential: pad thai, mango sticky rice, tom yum. Get Thai massage. Respect Buddha images and monks.
