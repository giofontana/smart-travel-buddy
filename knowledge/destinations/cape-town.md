# Cape Town

## Overview
South Africa's coastal gem between mountains and ocean. Three main areas: City Bowl, Atlantic Seaboard (beaches), Southern Suburbs. Stunning natural beauty with challenging inequality. English widely spoken. Very affordable for tourists. Mediterranean climate. Car recommended for exploring beyond city center.

## Best Time to Visit
November-March for summer and beach weather. December-January peak season and crowded. April-May or September-October for mild weather and wine harvest. Winter (June-August) cold and rainy but whale watching season.

## Neighborhoods
City Bowl: CBD, V&A Waterfront, shops. Camps Bay: Beach, restaurants, sunset views. Woodstock: Arts, hipster cafes, galleries. Sea Point: Coastal promenade. Constantia: Wine estates. Kalk Bay: Fishing village charm, antiques.

## Transportation
Car rental recommended for Table Mountain, Cape Point, wine routes. MyCiti bus serves waterfront and major routes - buy card at kiosks. Uber widely available and affordable. Trains exist but not recommended for tourists. Tours common for wine tasting and safaris.

## Must-See Spots
Table Mountain cableway (check wind conditions), Cape Point, Boulders Beach penguins, V&A Waterfront, Robben Island tour (book ahead), Kirstenbosch Botanical Gardens. Wine tasting in Stellenbosch and Franschhoek. Try bobotie, biltong, bunny chow. Township tours available for cultural context. Beach sunsets spectacular.
