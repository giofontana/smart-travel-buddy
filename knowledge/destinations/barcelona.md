# Barcelona

## Overview
Catalonia's capital on Mediterranean coast. Distinct identity from rest of Spain - Catalan language and culture strong. Gaudi architecture defines the cityscape. Beach city with urban energy. Layout based on grid (Eixample) plus medieval Gothic Quarter. Siesta culture affects shop hours.

## Best Time to Visit
May-June or September-October for warm weather without peak summer crowds. August is hot and locals vacation. Winter mild but some beach businesses close. La Merce festival in September features cultural celebrations.

## Neighborhoods
Gothic Quarter: Medieval streets, cathedral, tapas bars. Eixample: Modernist architecture, Gaudi buildings, upscale shopping. El Born: Trendy, Picasso Museum. Barceloneta: Beach, seafood. Gracia: Bohemian, local life. Montjuic: Hill with museums and Olympic sites.

## Transportation
Metro efficient and affordable - T10 ticket covers 10 rides. Bus network extensive. Las Ramblas walkable but watch for pickpockets. Bike lanes expanding. Taxis reasonable. Airport connected by train and bus. Most attractions within metro reach.

## Must-See Spots
Sagrada Familia (book timed entry weeks ahead), Park Guell (timed entry), Casa Batllo, La Boqueria market, Gothic Quarter wander. Beaches decent but crowded in summer. Tapas culture: small plates at multiple bars. Try jamon iberico, pan con tomate, vermouth. Dinner starts 9pm or later.
