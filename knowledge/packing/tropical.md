# Packing for Tropical Destinations

## Essentials
Passport with 6+ months validity, copies of documents, travel insurance, credit cards (inform bank), local currency small bills, unlocked phone, power adapter, reusable water bottle, dry bag for beaches, sunglasses with UV protection.

## Clothing
Lightweight, breathable fabrics - linen, cotton, moisture-wicking synthetics. Shorts, t-shirts, sundresses, swimwear (2 sets to rotate), light long pants/skirt for temples, light long sleeves for sun/mosquitos, wide-brimmed hat, comfortable walking sandals, flip-flops for beach, closed shoes for evenings. Pack light - can buy cheaply locally.

## Health
High SPF reef-safe sunscreen, insect repellent with DEET or picaridin, anti-diarrheal medication, oral rehydration salts, antihistamines, basic first aid, any prescriptions in original containers, mosquito net if staying budget accommodation, hand sanitizer, wet wipes.

## Electronics
Phone with international plan or local SIM, portable charger, waterproof phone case, e-reader loaded with books, headphones, travel adapter, camera if not using phone. Keep electronics in waterproof bag during boat trips.
