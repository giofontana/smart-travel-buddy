# Packing for City Break

## Essentials
Passport/ID, travel insurance, credit cards and some cash, phone with maps downloaded offline, power bank, reusable water bottle, small daypack or crossbody bag, city transit card or contactless payment card, umbrella (compact), guidebook or saved articles.

## Clothing
Comfortable walking shoes broken in (you'll walk miles daily), second pair for backup/evenings, 2-3 pants/jeans, 3-4 tops that mix and match, one smart outfit for nice restaurants/theater, light jacket or cardigan, weather-appropriate outer layer. Black jeans work everywhere. Avoid overpacking - cities have laundry.

## Health
Any prescriptions, pain reliever for sore feet, blister plasters, hand sanitizer, basic first aid, allergy medication if needed. Comfortable shoes prevent most issues.

## Electronics
Phone and charger, portable battery (essential for navigation and photos), headphones for transit, camera if not using phone, travel adapter for your destination, e-reader or tablet. Consider hotel safe for valuables when sightseeing.
