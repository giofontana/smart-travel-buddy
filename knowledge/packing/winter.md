# Packing for Winter Destinations

## Essentials
Passport, travel insurance, credit cards, local currency, phone, power adapter, reusable water bottle, thermal flask for hot drinks, lip balm with SPF (sun reflects off snow), hand warmers for extreme cold, moisturizer for dry air.

## Clothing
Layering system critical: base layer (thermal underwear, merino wool), mid layer (fleece, sweater), outer layer (waterproof insulated jacket). Warm pants, jeans, thermal leggings under pants if very cold. Waterproof boots with good traction, warm socks (wool or synthetic), gloves (waterproof outer, warm liner), scarf, beanie/hat covering ears. Pack coat-check bag for museums/restaurants.

## Health
Sunscreen (still needed in snow), moisturizer and lip balm (heating dries skin), any prescriptions, pain reliever, blister plasters for walking, hand warmers, tissues. Hydration important despite cold.

## Electronics
Phone (battery drains faster in cold - keep close to body), portable charger, camera, headphones, travel adapter. Bring cases/bags that work with gloved hands. Consider waterproof protection for snow.
