# Packing for Beach Vacation

## Essentials
Passport, travel insurance, credit cards and cash, phone, sunglasses (polarized for water glare), waterproof bag or case for beach, reusable water bottle, beach towel (or rent at hotel), flip-flops, sun hat, reef-safe sunscreen (required many destinations).

## Clothing
Swimwear (at least 2 so one can dry), cover-up for walking to/from beach, shorts, t-shirts, sundress or casual dress, sandals, one nicer outfit for dinner, light layers for air-conditioned restaurants/planes. Rash guard for extended sun exposure or snorkeling.

## Health
High SPF reef-safe sunscreen (reapply frequently), after-sun lotion or aloe, insect repellent for evenings, anti-diarrheal medication, motion sickness tablets if doing boat trips, waterproof bandages, any prescriptions. Hydration critical in sun.

## Electronics
Phone in waterproof case, portable charger, waterproof camera or GoPro for underwater, e-reader for beach reading, headphones, travel adapter. Keep electronics away from sand - bring ziplock bags.
