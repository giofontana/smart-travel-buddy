# Spain - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Dos besos (two kisses) standard among friends and women. Men shake hands or hug. Greet shopkeepers always. Siesta culture real - shops close 2-5pm. Dinner very late (9-11pm). Punctuality relaxed for social events.

## Tipping
Not expected but rounding up appreciated. Leave small change at bars and cafes. Restaurants 5-10% for good service optional. Taxi drivers appreciate rounding up. Service staff paid living wage unlike US.

## Dining
Tapas culture: order multiple small plates to share. Free tapas with drinks in some regions. Lunch is main meal (2-4pm). Dinner late and lighter. Don't expect fast service - meals are leisurely. Standing at bar cheaper than sitting. Throwing napkins on floor in some taverns traditional.

## Dress Codes
Casual but stylish. Avoid athletic wear outside gym. Beach attire stays at beach. Cover up in churches. Evening paseo (stroll) is dressed-up social time. Regional variation - Barcelona more casual than Madrid.

## Taboos
Being loud and demanding. Expecting early dinner. Wearing shoes indoors in homes. Criticizing local customs. Comparing Spain to Latin America. Assuming bullfighting is universally loved. Mistaking Catalan/Basque culture for Spanish.

## Useful Phrases
Por favor (please), gracias (thanks), perdon (excuse me), no hablo espanol (I don't speak Spanish), la cuenta por favor (check please), donde esta el bano (where's bathroom), habla ingles (speak English), vale (okay).
