# USA - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Handshakes common - firm grip expected. First names used quickly, even with strangers. Eye contact shows confidence and honesty. Personal space larger than Europe - arm's length distance. Casual, friendly demeanor standard.

## Tipping
Mandatory, not optional. Restaurants 15-20% pretax. Bars $1-2 per drink. Taxis 15-20%. Hotel housekeeping $2-5 per night. Delivery drivers 15-20%. Hair salons 15-20%. Withholding tip signals very poor service. Many workers rely on tips for income.

## Dining
Portions are huge. Doggy bags normal and expected. Free water and refills standard. Split checks common - just ask server. Casual dining dress accepted most places. Eating while walking fine. Dinner often early (6-7pm) compared to Europe.

## Dress Codes
Very casual overall. Athletic wear acceptable in public. Jeans standard. Most restaurants accept casual dress. Some upscale venues require business casual. Regional variation - coasts more fashion conscious, middle America very casual.

## Taboos
Discussing politics and religion with strangers increasingly sensitive. Smoking heavily restricted - designated areas only. Not tipping. Cutting in line (queue jumping). Asking age or salary.

## Useful Phrases
Regional greetings vary. "How are you" is greeting, not real question - answer "good, thanks" not detailed response. "Y'all" in South, "you guys" elsewhere. "Excuse me" for getting attention or apologizing.
