# France - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Always say "bonjour" when entering shops and "au revoir" when leaving. Kiss on cheeks (la bise) among friends but handshake for formal. Address as Monsieur/Madame unless invited to use first names. Failure to greet considered very rude.

## Tipping
Service (15%) included in bill by law. Round up or leave small change (1-2 euros) for good service. At cafes, leave coins. Taxi drivers appreciate rounding up fare. Not expected but appreciated for exceptional service.

## Dining
Keep hands on table (not lap) but not elbows. Bread placed on table, not plate. Don't cut salad with knife. Finish everything on plate. Lunch 12-2pm, dinner after 8pm. Don't ask for doggy bags. Splitting bills (l'addition) accepted but not common among French.

## Dress Codes
Dress well - French value style. Avoid athletic wear outside gym. Smart casual minimum for restaurants. Cover shoulders and knees in churches. No shorts for men in upscale venues.

## Taboos
Speaking loudly in public. Drinking coffee with meals (it's after). Expecting shops open Sundays or during lunch. Asking personal questions immediately. Assuming everyone speaks English.

## Useful Phrases
S'il vous plait (please), merci (thanks), pardon (excuse me), je ne parle pas francais (I don't speak French), l'addition s'il vous plait (check please), ou sont les toilettes (where's bathroom).
