# Brazil - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Very warm and physical. Kisses on both cheeks standard (sometimes one in Sao Paulo, three in Belo Horizonte). Hugs and back pats among men. Close personal space. Eye contact important. First names used immediately. "Tudo bem?" (all good?) standard greeting - answer "tudo" (all good).

## Tipping
10% service charge often included (check for "servico"). If not, 10% expected. Round up taxi fare. Hotel staff few reais. Street parking attendants (flanelinhas) 2-5 reais. Bar/restaurant 10% if no service charge. Beauty salons 10-15%.

## Dining
Late meals - lunch after 12:30pm, dinner after 8pm. Rodizio (all-you-can-eat) restaurants use card system: green=more, red=stop. Feijoada (black bean stew) traditional Saturday lunch. Acai bowls everywhere. Cafezinho (small strong coffee) offered constantly. Sharing pizza/dishes common. Don't order cappuccino - "cafe com leite" is standard.

## Dress Codes
Beach culture dominant - minimal clothing at beach. Havaianas (flip-flops) everywhere. Dress well for nights out - Brazilians care about appearance. Avoid shorts in upscale restaurants. Clubbing requires stylish dress. Casual elsewhere but put-together.

## Taboos
OK hand gesture offensive (avoid). Discussing poverty/crime insensitively. Comparing Brazil to Spanish-speaking countries (speak Portuguese). Expecting punctuality - flexible time culture. Walking alone at night in unfamiliar areas. Displaying expensive items openly.

## Useful Phrases
Oi/ola (hi), obrigado/a (thanks - o=male, a=female), por favor (please), com licenca (excuse me), nao falo portugues (I don't speak Portuguese), quanto custa (how much), onde fica (where is), fala ingles (speak English), valeu (thanks/cool).
