# Australia - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Very casual and friendly. "G'day mate" or "how ya going" standard. First names used immediately, even with strangers. Firm handshakes. Tall poppy syndrome - don't boast or act superior. Self-deprecating humor valued. Egalitarian culture - everyone treated equally.

## Tipping
Not expected or required - living wages paid. Rounding up appreciated. Upscale restaurants 10% for exceptional service. Taxi drivers keep change. No pressure to tip. Culture slowly changing but still minimal compared to US.

## Dining
BBQ culture strong - often BYO drinks. Brunch huge - cafes packed weekends. Coffee culture serious - flat whites standard. Asian food influences strong. Meat pies national snack. Fish and chips at beach tradition. Vegemite is acquired taste. Splitting bills (going Dutch) normal.

## Dress Codes
Very casual overall. Thongs (flip-flops) acceptable most places. Beach culture dominant - swimwear common. Some clubs require closed-toe shoes. Business casual for work. No shoes no service some venues. Sun protection essential - slip slop slap (sunscreen culture).

## Taboos
Taking yourself too seriously. Littering or not respecting nature. Drinking and driving strictly enforced. Swimming outside flagged areas dangerous. Not knowing how to handle drop bears (joke tourists fall for). Complaining about heat/sun (you chose to visit).

## Useful Phrases
Arvo (afternoon), servo (gas station), bottle-o (liquor store), brekkie (breakfast), no worries (you're welcome), cheers (thanks), reckon (think), yeah nah (no), nah yeah (yes), fair dinkum (really/genuine), she'll be right (it'll be okay).
