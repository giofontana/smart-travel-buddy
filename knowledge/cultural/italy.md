# Italy - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Handshakes or kisses on both cheeks among friends. Always greet shopkeepers with "buongiorno" (morning) or "buonasera" (evening). Eye contact important. Italians expressive and animated - don't take raised voices as anger.

## Tipping
Not expected but appreciated. Service (coperto) charge often on bill for bread/table. Round up bill or leave 5-10% for good service. Bartenders get small change. Not tipping is acceptable but rude for excellent service.

## Dining
Cappuccino only before 11am - espresso after meals. Pasta is first course, not main. Don't ask for parmesan on seafood pasta. Lunch 1-3pm, dinner 8pm or later. Splitting bills unusual - one person pays. Don't rush meals - dining is social. Standing at bar cheaper than table service.

## Dress Codes
Italians dress well - la bella figura matters. Avoid beachwear away from beach. Cover shoulders and knees in churches (scarves provided). No shorts for men in fine dining. Sneakers acceptable but not athletic wear.

## Taboos
Ordering cappuccino after meals. Asking for substitutions or changes to dishes. Putting feet on furniture. Being loud in churches. Expecting fast service. Walking into churches during mass as tourist.

## Useful Phrases
Per favore (please), grazie (thanks), scusi (excuse me), non parlo italiano (I don't speak Italian), il conto per favore (check please), dov'e il bagno (where's bathroom), parla inglese (speak English).
