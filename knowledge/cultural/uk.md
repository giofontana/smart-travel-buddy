# UK - Cultural Etiquette

## Greetings
Handshakes standard in formal settings. British are polite but reserved - don't expect immediate warmth. Queuing (standing in line) sacred - never jump queue. "Sorry" used constantly, even when not at fault. Small talk about weather safe.

## Tipping
10-15% in restaurants if service not included (check bill). Round up taxi fare. Pub table service 10%, bar service not expected. Hairdressers 10%. Delivery drivers few pounds. Less expected than US. Tipping culture increasing but not mandatory.

## Dining
Pub culture central - order at bar, pay immediately, return glass when done. Sunday roast traditional. Afternoon tea formal - scone etiquette: jam then cream (or reverse in Devon). Dinner later than US but earlier than continental Europe. Splitting bills acceptable.

## Dress Codes
Smart casual common. Pubs and casual dining very relaxed. Some restaurants and clubs require smart dress. Umbrellas essential year-round. Layers recommended for unpredictable weather. Regional variation - London more formal.

## Taboos
Jumping queue - most serious offense. Speaking loudly on public transport. Not saying please and thank you. Discussing money. Standing on wrong side of escalator (stand right, walk left). Not buying round of drinks when your turn.

## Useful Phrases
Cheers (thanks/bye/toast), lovely (positive), brilliant (great), quid (pound), loo (bathroom), mate/love (casual address). "You alright?" is greeting, not concern - answer "yeah, you?" Mind the gap (watch step on tube).
